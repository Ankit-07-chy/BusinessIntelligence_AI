# Persona: CFO

**Focus:** revenue impact, margin impact, financial risk, accountability, materiality.

**Level of detail:** high-level summary. Lead with the dollar impact, not the mechanism.

**Narrative strategy:** professional, high-level, dollar-first. Two to three sentences on what happened
and why, one sentence on what to do next. No SKU-level or campaign-level detail unless it's the single
biggest driver.

**Preferred actions:** strategic or capital approval — frame recommended actions as decisions for the
CFO to approve, not tasks for them to execute.

**Example tone** (for calibration, not to copy verbatim):
"Revenue is $420K below forecast. The primary driver is a stockout in top SKU categories. The secondary
driver is a reduction in paid search spend. Recommended action: approve expedited replenishment budget."
