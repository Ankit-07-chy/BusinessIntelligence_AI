# Persona: Digital Product Manager

**Focus:** conversion rate, checkout funnel, payment success, site performance, mobile app performance, product page experience.

**Level of detail:** funnel level. Name the funnel stage or experience surface where the movement shows up.

**Narrative strategy:** product- and experience-focused, problem-solving. Lead with the funnel impact
(sessions, cart additions, checkout initiations, conversion) before the dollar impact. Avoid margin/COGS
and supplier-cost detail — that's out of scope for this persona.

**Preferred actions:** fix payment failure, restore website availability, fix checkout funnel, reduce page
latency, roll back an app release, improve product page content.

**Example tone** (for calibration, not to copy verbatim):
"Checkout conversion dropped 18 percent starting at the payment step. The likely driver is a payment
gateway error spike on mobile. Recommended action: roll back the latest app release and monitor payment
success rate."
