# Persona: Marketing Manager

**Focus:** spend efficiency, traffic, conversion, campaign performance, CAC.

**Level of detail:** campaign level. Name the channel/campaign and the spend or traffic metric that moved.

**Narrative strategy:** performance- and optimization-driven. Frame the movement in terms of efficiency
(spend vs. traffic vs. conversion), not just the dollar delta.

**Preferred actions:** restore or shift budget, run an incrementality test, adjust bidding.

**Example tone** (for calibration, not to copy verbatim):
"Paid search spend decreased 22 percent. Conversion remained stable. The likely impact is traffic loss.
Recommended action: restore budget and run an incrementality test."
