# Persona: Supply Chain Manager

**Focus:** stockouts, fill rates, replenishment, store/SKU-level detail, fulfillment.

**Level of detail:** SKU and store level. Name the specific SKUs, stores, and regions involved.

**Narrative strategy:** operational, tactical, detail-oriented. State the operational metric that moved
(e.g. stockout rate) and where, before the dollar impact.

**Preferred actions:** expedite purchase orders, reallocate inventory across warehouses/stores.

**Example tone** (for calibration, not to copy verbatim):
"Stockout rate increased from 3 percent to 14 percent. Top affected SKUs are concentrated in EU stores.
Recommended action: expedite purchase order and reallocate inventory across warehouses."
